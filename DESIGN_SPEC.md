# Sam and the Mopoke — locked Doom II design baseline

## Route
One continuous authored MAP01:
house → playground → black-gum forest → cemetery → tomb → underground station → cursed shopping centre → impossible library → Mopoke rupture.

## Doom level-design standard
The map must use Doom II's design grammar without copying commercial maps: readable landmarks, internal rooms and lanes, loops and return sightlines, height/material changes, optional pockets, ambushes from formerly safe space, meaningful resource decisions, and action peaks separated by dread/recovery.

## Environment
No single generic floor, wall or ceiling across the game.

- House: carpet/timber/tile, domestic plaster, timber kitchen, pink bathroom, enclosed porch/carport and corrugated shed.
- Playground: grass/open sky, fence/equipment sightline breaks.
- Forest: dirt/open sky, dark bark/deadfall and forked lanes.
- Cemetery: grave-earth/open sky, weathered masonry and mausoleum pockets.
- Tomb: lower sandstone floor/ceiling, compressed chambers.
- Station: concrete/platform floor, tiled/concrete walls, enclosed roof.
- Mall: polished/grimy commercial tile, shopfront/panel walls, broad ceiling.
- Library: dark timber/carpet-like floor, shelves/plaster walls, low oppressive ceiling.
- Outdoor areas use the custom night sky.
- Wall textures bake in trims, grime, damage, cracks or signage motifs so locations remain readable at mobile resolution.

## Monsters
Enemy roster must include:
Mopoke Shambler, Undead Goat, Rot Possum, Cursed Crow, Station Husk, Mall Brute, Library Shade, and the final Mopoke.
They must not all share the same movement/combat role.

## Weapons
Dad begins only with damaged Zombie Hands. All 13 cursed weapons are physical discoveries in the world:
Demon Staff, Forbidden Amulet, Zombie Fire Virus, Cursed Doll, Blood of Tim Shakomontus, Statue of Feeb, Squeeze Baby of Dacron, Scooter, Gauntlet of Stones, Feather Wand, Possessed Sausage, Jar of Dad Toenails, Unholy Jandal.
Each weapon needs a distinct pickup/first-person silhouette, attack rhythm, recoil/animation and sound identity.
The Gauntlet of Stones is a large articulated GOLD glove with coloured gemstones and the costly heavy blast.

## Progression and story
Goat Finger → tomb gate; Crow Finger → station service gate; Possum Finger → archive gate.
Every mandatory finger is obtainable before its gate.
Sam pursuit, mundane clues, supernatural false trails, Lincoln notes, survivor hints, searchable loot and story narration are location-driven rather than timer-driven.
Dad remembers dying; he can think/speak internally, but Sam hears only zombie noises.
Lincoln remains traces/foreshadowing rather than a normal encounter.

## Encounter rhythm
House = dread.
Playground/forest = exposed pursuit and uncertainty.
Cemetery/tomb = compression.
Station = first major action spike.
Mall = largest sustained fight.
Library = sudden quiet, maze pressure, then final escalation.
Recovery caches belong after peaks rather than before every encounter.

## Non-negotiables
Real Doom-format game data, no generic custom raycaster, no commercial Doom II IWAD data, no permanent waypoint, no bait/trap/adrenaline survival UI, no placeholder-shaped finger keys, no menu-granted cursed arsenal, and no progression softlocks.
